Scope, data, metric definitions, and methodology

Data integrity

Every measured record is checked for source hash, cohort hash, denominator, explicit missingness, evidence class, and leakage constraints.

Comparison integrity

Direct deltas require equivalent dataset, partition, baseline, policy, prompt, model, metric version, eligibility, leakage class, and evidence class except for the declared treatment.

Statistical integrity

Safe N=0 forces null empirical fields. Positive formal improvement later requires paired unseen evidence, positive net-correction confidence bounds, exact McNemar p<0.05, no macro-F1 decline, and no predefined subgroup harm.

Limitations and robustness

  • Paper-versus-current counts are contextual version markers, not accuracy deltas.
  • Offline topology and fault fixtures do not establish live or universal behavior.
  • Latency is machine-specific; the latest EXP-036 run meets its ratio limits, while accepted history retains a prior miss.
  • Human-value and classification experiments remain unexecuted until their consent and evidence gates open.
  • Negative and null results remain reportable and cannot be silently retuned away.
