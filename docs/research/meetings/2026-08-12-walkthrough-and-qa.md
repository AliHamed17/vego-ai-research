## Part 3 — Anticipated questions and honest answers

### On the gap and the questions

**"Is this actually novel?"**
Not established, and the chapter does not claim it is. The searches are frozen and unrun, so every
"no established approach does X" is labelled a candidate claim for Chapter 2 to confirm or refute.
What *is* defensible today: four bodies of work each address a part, and the seed corpus contains
nothing that connects selective intervention, governed capture, and scope-aware reuse.

**"Silva et al. already did human-in-the-loop LLM domain modelling — how are you different?"**
That is the nearest neighbour in the seed corpus, and full text has not been read yet, so the honest
answer is: the distinctness claim is pending. At title level it positions the human *inside the
modelling activity*; this proposal asks when a system should *decide to interrupt*, what is retained
afterwards, and whether it may be reused elsewhere. Reading it properly is a Chapter 2 task.

**"Why three sub-questions? Are they sufficient?"**
They cover capture, governance, and use. Whether they are *jointly sufficient* for "reliable human–AI
co-reasoning" is stated in §3.8 as an open decomposition question — none of the three takes
reliability itself as its object. That is a genuine question for this meeting, not a hole I missed.

**"Isn't this just describing VEGO-AI?"**
No, and §3.9 is written to prevent that reading: VEGO-AI is the motivating case and the vehicle, the
question is the contribution, and Chapter 4 treats it as one of two instantiation contexts. If any
part of the chapter reads as a product pitch, that is a defect I want pointed out.

**"You use 'variability' — but that word already means something in SPL research."**
Correct, and §3.2.4 addresses it explicitly: that tradition studies *designed* variability;
this thesis studies *observed interpretive* variability. Same word, different phenomenon, and the
formalism does not transfer. The construct is defined properly in Chapter 2.

### On evidence

**"What can you actually claim today?"**
Mechanism, architecture, traceability and readiness in the software/modelling domain. Not accuracy,
not generalization, not effort reduction, not anything clinical. §3.11 states this as a table.

**"When do the labels arrive?"**
That is the binding constraint and it is not solvable by more building. It needs two independent
reviewers and an adjudicator; the resourcing question is open (see the decisions sheet). Until then
Study 3's quantitative arm cannot start.

**"What if the results are negative?"**
Each question is falsifiable and a negative result is reportable — §3.11 says what failure means for
each. That is deliberate: a bounded negative finding about when human judgment can be reused is a
contribution, not a failed project.
