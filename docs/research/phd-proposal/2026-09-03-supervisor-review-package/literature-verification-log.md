## Top-down synthesis

The established mechanisms form six streams: selective prediction and deferral; reviewer competence and assignment; human reliance and interaction; provenance and reviewability; corrective memory and case reuse; and transfer, authorization, and lifecycle control. The literature supports each mechanism separately and several pairwise combinations. It therefore refutes any claim that human intervention, expert routing, provenance, memory, contextual adaptation, or revocation is individually novel.

## Bottom-up reconciliation with the frozen VEGO-AI errors

The frozen outputs expose a different unit of analysis from most comparator studies: a **claim about a concrete model fragment at a named pipeline stage**. Recorded review shows frequent disagreement in precisely two places: whether a generated guideline is acceptable and whether an uncovered fragment is a valid alternative. Those observations do not establish an error rate because the review was neither independent nor randomly sampled. They do establish that the implementation needs an explicit place to request and record a judgment.

The residual gap is therefore an **integration-and-evaluation gap**, not component novelty: within the verified corpus, no evaluated approach was found that simultaneously (1) routes a claim to a reviewer using separately represented competence and authority under a bounded attention budget, (2) retains the answer with rationale, evidence, dissent, provenance, and lifecycle state, and (3) allows later influence only after authorization and context-applicability checks, with a use receipt. This remains a supported proposal-stage synthesis, not an exhaustive field-wide absence claim. It is falsified if a directly comparable evaluated system is found.

## Search and verification limitations

- This update used targeted query families and adversarial named-target searches; it did not complete the registered multi-database review.
- Citation counts were not used as evidence.
- ArXiv-only systems such as MemOS remain preprints and are described as such.
- The 2026 VEGO-AI foundation manuscript is a supplied working manuscript with placeholder publication metadata and is not counted as externally verified.
- Search and screening remain subject to supervisor review and a second reviewer before any systematic-review claim.
