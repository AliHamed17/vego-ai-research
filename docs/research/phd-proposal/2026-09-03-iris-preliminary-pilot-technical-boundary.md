- Any use of the sealed holdout before the development policy is frozen and approved.

- That 178/26 and 179/27 are interchangeable denominators or an improvement.
- That current M4B-1 improves results; it changes 0/27 classifications.
- That same-pattern memory is generalization evidence.
- That a trigger count is a verified error count, or that a queued case benefits
  from human intervention.
- That the pilot, requirements, labels, Hebrew meaning, or claims are approved
  before the relevant human review.
