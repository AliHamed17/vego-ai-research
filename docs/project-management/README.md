# Project Management

Strategic planning, risks, and roadmap documentation.

## Planning & tracking

- \oadmap.md\ — high-level research roadmap with milestones, phases, and key deliverables
- \isk-register.md\ — project risks, mitigation strategies, and contingency plans

## Purpose

These files track the project's strategic direction, resource allocation, and risk mitigation independent of the detailed experiment/evidence registry. Use them for:

- Long-term planning and dependency tracking
- Risk identification and early mitigation
- Communicating progress to supervisors and stakeholders
